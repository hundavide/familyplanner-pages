Family Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Family Planner is a privately operated, self-hosted scheduling application intended for use by authorized family members. This notice describes its use of Google Calendar data.

Information accessed

With the Google account holder's authorization, Family Planner accesses Google Calendar information needed to list available calendars and create, read, update, or delete calendar events. Event information may include titles, dates, times, locations, descriptions, and calendar identifiers.

Purpose of use

Google Calendar data is used to synchronize family schedules and manage events selected within Family Planner. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and sharing

Family Planner operates on a privately managed server. Application configuration, calendar mappings, and synchronization information may be stored on that server. Calendar data is not sold or shared with advertisers. Data is transferred to Google only as necessary to perform the authorized Google Calendar operations.

Access and retention

Access is restricted to authorized users of the private application. Stored data is retained as needed to operate the family planner and may be deleted by the application administrator, subject to any backups.

Revoking access

Google Calendar access can be revoked at any time through Google Account third-party connections. To request deletion of locally stored data, contact the application administrator.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: hundavide@gmail.com
